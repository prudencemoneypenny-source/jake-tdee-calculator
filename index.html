What is TDEE? TDEE stands for Total Daily Energy Expenditure — a fancy way of saying “roughly how many calories this body burns in a normal day, including breathing, blinking, training, walking to the kettle and pretending stairs are cardio.” It is an estimate, not a prophecy carved into a protein shaker.
